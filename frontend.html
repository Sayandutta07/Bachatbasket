A product listed at a lower base price on Platform A often results in a higher final cart value once delivery, handling fees, and threshold penalties are applied.

Daily urban quick-commerce shoppers, budget-conscious households, students, and busy professionals across Indian tier-1 and tier-2 cities are most affected.
